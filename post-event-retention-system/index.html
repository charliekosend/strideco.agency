FAQ

Questions before you book a call.

What happens if we don't hit the re-registration target?

You pay £500 less than the fixed fee. We agree the target together before we start, based on your historical re-registration rate, so it's realistic rather than a moving goalpost.

How is the 10% performance fee calculated?

Against revenue generated above the agreed baseline in the measurement window — not your total event revenue. We agree the baseline and window upfront, before any work starts.

What access do you need for the audit?

Read access to your participant database or CRM, plus any post-event comms you're already sending. If you've already got an ESP or CRM in place, we work inside it — no forced migrations. If you don't have one yet, that's a quick separate conversation on the call rather than something baked into the fixed fee.

How soon will we see results?

The sequence goes live within the 4-week build window. The re-registration lift itself shows up at your next registration window, measured clearly against your baseline.

What if we've never sent a post-event sequence before?

That's the most common starting point, not a blocker — the database audit is built to work from zero.

Do we need a long-term contract?

No. This is a fixed 4-week engagement. What happens after is up to you — some clients stop here, most bring us in on more.
