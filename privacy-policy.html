When you use the "Book a call" form, we collect the name, work email address, and event/company details you provide. We don't ask for or knowingly collect any other personal data through this site.

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we start working together, for the duration of that relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards for legal and accounting purposes. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 7).

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). To exercise any of these rights, contact contact@strideco.agency.
